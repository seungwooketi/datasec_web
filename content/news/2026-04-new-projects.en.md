New government R&D programmes began in April 2026. The center is the **lead institute on three** of them.

- [3D Spatial Reconstruction and Visualization for Low-Visibility and Non-Visible Environments](/en/research/low-visibility-3d/) — lead
- [Personalized Sensory Information Sharing and Interaction Across Heterogeneous Devices](/en/research/cross-device-sensory-sharing/) — lead
- [Data Hub Platform for Marine Batteries](/en/research/marine-battery-data-hub/) — lead
- [Automatic Sound Generation and Spatial-Analysis Rendering for Virtual Spaces](/en/research/virtual-space-audio/)

The list is on the [Research](/en/research/) page.
