Two further research programmes funded by the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy (MOTIE) and the Korea Evaluation Institute of Industrial Technology (KEIT) have been awarded.

- **Knowledge Service Industry Technology Development Programme** — *AI-BOM Based Reliability Testing for Next-Generation Powertrain Systems* (Jongseol Lee, Chief Researcher). VINA FCM is the lead institute.
- **K-On-Device AI Semiconductor Technology Development Programme** — sub-project 3, *Development of On-Device AI Semiconductor-Based AI Algorithms, Intelligence Interconnection Framework, and Software Platforms for AI Operations* (Jaejong Song, Principal Researcher). Gamba Labs leads sub-project 3, working with LG Electronics as the overall lead institute.

Across both programmes the center takes on **AI-BOM based verification that an AI service behaves safely**, and **a platform that provides an inference environment across a range of on-device SoCs**.

The full list of current programmes is on the [Research](/en/research/) page.
