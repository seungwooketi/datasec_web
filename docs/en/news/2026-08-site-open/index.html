The center's website is now open

The center's website is now public, in Korean and English. The language control in the header switches the whole site.

The values on Research and People are not retyped by hand — they are carried over from the source-of-truth files the center maintains. When those files are updated, the site follows.

What stays off the site is also settled: account numbers, budgets, monthly effort allocations and employee numbers are not published.
