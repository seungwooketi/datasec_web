AEGIS-RM awarded: securing the intelligence inside factory robots

A joint proposal with Seoul National University, ENKI WhiteHat, Roboworks and Kyoungwoo Precision has been selected for MoTIR funding. Over four years we will build technology that protects the models of manufacturing robots and the process know-how they learn.

Related project AEGIS-RM

The center joins two programmes funded by MOTIE and KEIT, taking on AI-BOM based safety verification of AI services and an inference platform for on-device SoCs.
