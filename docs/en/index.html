AI Research Division

Element and system technologies for safe and secure AI services

The AI Data and Security Research Center at the Korea Electronics Technology Institute (KETI) works on advanced use of data to improve AI performance, on edge operation, on multimodal data analysis, and on the transparency and safety of data and models.

Featured projects by topic
SECURITY

AI Safety

인공지능 안전

Running AI in a hardware-isolated space to protect models and data

We run robot AI inference at the edge without exposing it and keep its execution from being tampered with. We protect the manufacturing know-how inside models with irreversible learning and watermarking, and separate intelligence from control to detect abnormal behavior.

Featured projectIntelligence Protection and Secure Execution for AI Robots and Humanoids Safeguarding Core Manufacturing Technology (AEGIS-RM)MoTIR / Lead / 2026-08 – 2029-12
SECURITY

Physical Security

물리보안

Sensing and responding to on-site risks with video and sensors

We analyze video, sound and sensor data together to detect and respond to security and safety risks in buildings and industrial sites. We build security AI that runs on servers and lightweight edge devices, and an open video-security platform whose cameras protect themselves.

Featured projectIntelligent Security and Risk-Response System Based on Integrated Multimodal Data AnalysisMSIT / Lead / 2024-06 – 2027-12
DATA

Multimodal Data

멀티모달 데이터

Understanding and linking data of different forms and sources

We build AI that analyzes video, sound, sensor data and text together, and verify the quality of multimodal retrieval-augmented generation. We also fuse heterogeneous data from scattered sources and share it robustly over unstable networks.

Featured projectIntelligent Security and Risk-Response System Based on Integrated Multimodal Data AnalysisMSIT / Lead / 2024-06 – 2027-12
DATA

Content

콘텐츠

AI-generated digital humans, sound and 3D space for immersive content

We develop models that generate human appearance and motion, and technology that creates sound effects and ambience to match a scene. We also reconstruct low-visibility spaces in 3D and personalize hearing and touch across different XR devices.

Featured project3D Spatial Reconstruction and Visualization for Low-Visibility and Non-Visible EnvironmentsMSIT / Lead / 2026-04 – 2028-12
